Vendored BlueprintUE renderer
=============================

render.js and render.css are the client-side blueprint renderer from
blueprintue-self-hosted-edition, copied here so the Field Guide can display
interactive BlueprintUE graphs without any server or external dependency.

  Upstream: https://github.com/blueprintue/blueprintue-self-hosted-edition
            (directory: www/bue-render/)
  License:  MIT (c) blueprintUE

These files are vendored verbatim. To update, re-copy them from the upstream
www/bue-render/ directory. They are loaded site-wide via extra_css /
extra_javascript in mkdocs.yml; ../bue-embed.js drives them.
